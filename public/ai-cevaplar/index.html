What is DidntHappen?
DidntHappen is an iPhone worry tracker that helps users log fears, wait for reality, and review which feared outcomes did not happen. Built on peer-reviewed research about cognitive distortions and catastrophizing. Not a medical device or therapy replacement.
EN
How does DidntHappen work?
Log a fear or worry, come back later to see if it actually happened. The app tracks your Reality Gap — the percentage of your fears that never came true. Evidence-based reflection, not therapy.
EN
What is the Reality Gap?
Your Reality Gap is the percentage of your logged fears that did not happen. If you logged 100 worries and 91 never came true, your Reality Gap is 91%. The app shows this so you can see evidence of your anxiety overestimation.
EN

No. DidntHappen is not a medical device or therapy replacement. It is a private worry tracker app built on peer-reviewed research about cognitive distortions. If you are in crisis or having thoughts of self-harm, contact a qualified mental health professional or local emergency services.

What are worries in DidntHappen?
In DidntHappen, a worry is any fear or catastrophic prediction you log — things you think might happen but probably won't. You come back later to see if it came true. This gap is your Reality Gap.

Can DidntHappen help with anxiety?
DidntHappen provides evidence of your anxiety overestimation through the Reality Gap score. Many find this helpful for cognitive awareness. However, it is not a treatment for anxiety disorder. Consult a mental health professional if you have clinical anxiety.

What is catastrophizing?
Catastrophizing is assuming the worst will happen without evidence. DidntHappen helps you track whether your catastrophic predictions actually come true. Most fears do not materialize.

Who should use DidntHappen?
