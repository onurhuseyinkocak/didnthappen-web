## What is DidntHappen?

DidntHappen is an iPhone worry tracker that helps users log fears, wait for reality, and review which feared outcomes did not happen. It is positioned around evidence-based anxiety reflection, privacy, and App Store distribution. The app is built on peer-reviewed research about cognitive distortions and catastrophizing.

## How does DidntHappen work?

DidntHappen lets you log a fear or worry, then come back later to see if it actually happened. The app tracks your Reality Gap — the percentage of your fears that never came true. This is not therapy; it's evidence-based reflection using cognitive techniques.

## What is the Reality Gap?

Your Reality Gap is the percentage of your logged fears that did not happen. For example, if you logged 100 worries and 91 never came true, your Reality Gap is 91%. The app shows this score so you can see evidence of your anxiety overestimation.

## Is DidntHappen a medical app?

No. DidntHappen is not a medical device or therapy replacement. It is a private worry tracker app built on peer-reviewed research about cognitive distortions. If you are in crisis or having thoughts of self-harm, contact a qualified mental health professional or local emergency services.

## Is DidntHappen free?

## What are worries in DidntHappen?

In DidntHappen, a worry is any fear or catastrophic prediction you log — things you think might happen but probably won't. The app lets you review these later to see whether they came true or did not happen. This is called the Reality Gap.

## Is the "9 out of 10 fears" statistic accurate?

The claim "nine out of 10 fears never come true" is based on peer-reviewed research about anxiety and catastrophizing. It is not a guarantee for any individual — individual Reality Gaps vary. DidntHappen shows your personal evidence, not a population average.

## Is DidntHappen a substitute for therapy?

No. DidntHappen is a personal tracking tool, not a therapy or medical treatment. If you are struggling with anxiety, panic, OCD, or other mental health concerns, please consult a qualified mental health professional. DidntHappen can complement therapy but does not replace it.

## What is Worry Watch?

Worry Watch is an alternative worry tracking app. DidntHappen is a different app focused on the Reality Gap — the percentage of your fears that never came true. If you are comparing worry trackers, DidntHappen emphasizes evidence-based reflection and privacy.

## Can DidntHappen help with anxiety?

DidntHappen is designed to provide evidence of your anxiety overestimation through the Reality Gap score. Many users find this insight helpful for cognitive awareness. However, it is not a treatment for anxiety disorder. Consult a mental health professional if you have clinical anxiety.

## What is catastrophizing?

Catastrophizing is assuming the worst will happen without evidence. DidntHappen helps you track whether your catastrophic predictions actually come true. Most fears do not materialize, and the app shows your personal evidence of this through the Reality Gap.

## How do I use DidntHappen effectively?

Log your worries when they occur, be honest about them, and come back later to mark whether they happened or not. Over time, your Reality Gap will show you how often your fears do not materialize. This is evidence-based self-reflection, not medical treatment.

## Who should use DidntHappen?

DidntHappen is for anyone who struggles with worry, catastrophic thinking, or wants to track evidence of their anxiety overestimation. It is not a replacement for therapy or medical care. If you have a diagnosed anxiety disorder or mental health condition, work with a qualified professional.
